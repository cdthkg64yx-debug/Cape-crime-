          Our Story

          IZHAI begins with the textiles of tradition. For Spring / Summer '26, the Madras check, heritage borders and tonal surface motifs are taken out of the archive and set within the clean lines of contemporary dressing.

          Forest green, burgundy and ivory are placed against one another in colour-blocked panels, check pockets and side stripes, while gold-toned borders trace hems, cuffs and waistbands. Wide-leg trousers and relaxed silhouettes give every look an ease that belongs to everyday life.

          From the everyday shirt to the floor-length drape, the intent stays the same: tradition reimagined for modern dressing, with garments that bring character to the wardrobe.
